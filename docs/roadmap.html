Study 1 — nine same-configuration arms

Three real tickets × three arms, futures unreachable by construction, nine of nine valid declarations, mean 143k tokens/arm. Finding: divergence is region-shaped — convergence where the environment decides, forks where the ticket leaves judgement open, and least-confident declarations co-locating with the forks.

Interruption surfaces under the effective-false-positive discipline

Any tool that points a human at a fork spends attention. Before shipping heuristic pointers we will measure their effective false-positive rate under Google's behavioural definition (action taken, or not), with the instrumentation logging flagged → acted / dismissed / ignored from day one.
